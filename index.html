How to know exactly when you can donate blood again

The week after you give blood, one question comes up: “when can I donate again?” The honest answer is a single date. donorbook computes it on your own device — from cited national rules — and never sends your health information anywhere.

The problem

Search “when can I donate blood again” and you get everything but a date.

Most results are a vague blog post, or a lead-generation page that wants your name, phone number and medical history before it tells you anything. The real answer is arithmetic anyone can do — last donation + your country's interval — and it should not cost you your privacy.

what you usually get
  • ✕ “It depends” blog posts with no date
  • ✕ Forms that harvest your details first

  • ✕ No sense of which rule applies to you

  • ✓ One exact next-eligible date

How it works

Your last donation, plus one published interval, becomes an exact date.

Pick your country and donation type. donorbook reads the interval straight from its cited ruleset and adds it to your last-donation date. The eligibility ring then fills up as the wait elapses — a live countdown to the day you can give again.

Every verdict is cited

No number appears without the rule it came from.

donorbook never authors a rule from memory. Each interval, age floor and deferral is quoted verbatim from a named authority — NBTC India, the American Red Cross, UK NHSBT — with a source link and the date it was last verified. And any medication or detailed-history question resolves to a single honest answer: ask the blood bank.

Privacy you can verify

The browser itself blocks any send. It is not a promise — it is enforced.

donorbook ships one line in its security policy: connect-src 'none'. That instructs your browser to refuse every network request the page could make — fetch, upload, analytics, all of it. Your donation history stays in this browser's storage and physically cannot leave.

A quick tour

Five things donorbook does — all on your device.

Next-date calculator

Country × donation type × sex × last date → the exact next-eligible date on the ring.

Guided pre-check

Age, weight, haemoglobin and a fixed set of deferrals → a verdict with its cited rule.

Private logbook

Date, type, centre, notes — stored only in your browser, with a yearly count vs the cap.

Group compatibility

Any of the 8 groups → donate-to / receive-from, for red cells or plasma.

Exports as the handoff
